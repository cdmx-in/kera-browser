# Privacy policy for Kera Browser

Kera Browser is a Chrome extension made by CodeMax for its team, based on [Nanobrowser](https://github.com/nanobrowser/nanobrowser) (Apache-2.0). This policy says what it does with your data.

## What leaves your browser

- The text of the pages you ask about or ask it to work on, your messages, and the screenshots the agent takes are sent to Kera (https://kera.cdmx.io), CodeMax's own Open WebUI, with your Kera API key, to run the model you chose. Nothing is sent to any other server.
- Kera applies your account's model access, records usage against your account, and keeps logs as described in CodeMax's internal policies.

## What stays in your browser

- Your Kera API key, your conversations, your settings and your per-site permissions are stored in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. They are never exported and never sent anywhere but Kera.
- The extension collects no analytics and has no uninstall survey.

## Permissions

- `debugger` and access to all sites: to read and act on the page in a tab the agent works on, only during a run you started.
- `tabs`, `tabGroups`, `sidePanel`, `storage`, `scripting`, `webNavigation`, `offscreen`, `alarms`, `contextMenus`, `notifications`, `downloads`: the side panel, the conversation's tab group, saved settings, the highlight on a page during a run, notifications when the agent needs you, and files it saves with your confirmation.
